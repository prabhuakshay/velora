# Velora

A personal budgeting app for one person, who organises money into categories and tracks where it goes.

## Language

**Category**:
A budget bucket that money is assigned to. Either income or expense.
_Avoid_: Tag, envelope

**Expense Account**:
Where money goes when it is spent, such as a shop or a landlord.
_Avoid_: Party, payee, counterparty, merchant, vendor

**Merge**:
Folding one Expense Account into another. The source disappears and everything that pointed at it points at the target.
_Avoid_: Combine, consolidate

**Activity Feed**:
The newest-first list of every change to Categories and Expense Accounts.
_Avoid_: Log, timeline, audit trail
