# Goals allocate what the user holds rather than earmarking money

A Goal counts a percentage or a fixed amount of Asset Accounts and Holdings toward it, and its Progress is what those Allocations are worth now. Investments are valued at Market Value. Firefly III's piggy banks, where the user moves amounts into a Goal by hand, were set aside. Most of what a Goal is saved in grows or falls on its own each night when Prices update, so a hand-entered amount would go stale the day after it was entered. Goals are views only. They never move money and never change a Balance, Market Value, Net Worth or Budget, in line with Budgets being limits rather than envelopes (ADR 0008). One Account or Holding's Allocations can't add up to more than all of it, so the same rupee is never counted toward two Goals.
