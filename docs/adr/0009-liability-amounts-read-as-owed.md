# Liability amounts read as what is owed

Every signed amount on an Asset or Liability Account is the change to its Balance, so for a Liability "+" is charged (more owed) and "−" is paid off (less owed), the same way on the Liabilities list, the Account's page and the Forecast; a payment that lowers the Balance is never shown as a plus because money moved in. Colour follows the direction that is good for the user rather than the sign: green marks an Asset going up, a Liability going down and income, the bad direction gets no colour, and red is kept for figures that are bad when negative, such as an Asset Balance, Net Worth or Budget left, so a Liability's Balance is never red. A single Liability whose Balance is below zero reads as "₹X in credit" rather than a negative number. On a card's page each row also says "Charged" or "Paid" to screen readers, the way Party rows say "Received" or "Spent". Under Privacy Mode these amounts show only the mask, with no sign, colour or "in credit", as each of those would give the value's direction away. One template tag library owns sign, colour and Privacy Mode, so no template builds its own.
