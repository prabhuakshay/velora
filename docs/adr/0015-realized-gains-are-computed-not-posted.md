# Realized Gains are worked out from Lots, not posted to the ledger

A Sell's Realized Gain is worked out by the holdings layer from the Lots it took, and nothing is posted to an Income or Expense Account for it. Posting it was set aside because a loss would need a Split into an Income Account, which ADR 0003's direction rules forbid. Posting losses to an Expense Account instead would make Budgets, Spent and Unbudgeted all filter that Account out, and one forgotten filter shows wrong numbers. An Investment Account's Balance therefore stays the money moved into it less what came out, and its Cash is worked out as its Balance less the Cost Basis still held, plus the Realized Gain of Sells since its Opening Balance date. Income reports never show capital gains, and the Investments pages report them instead. Dividends are real money arriving, so they are ordinary Income Transactions.
