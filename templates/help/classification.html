Classification is where you keep your Parties and Tags. A Party is who money goes to or comes from: a shop, an employer, a landlord or a friend. A Tag is a name you put on Splits to group them across Parties and Accounts, such as a trip. Each Party and Tag has its own page showing everything recorded with it.

{% endblock %} {% block setup %}
  • One Party per business or person, not per branch or app. Asha's grocery shopping at any Nature's Basket store is all Party "Nature's Basket".
  • Use the name you'd search for, short and plain: "Acme Technologies", not "ACME TECH PVT LTD SAL CR".
  • Keep Tags few and short-lived: a trip, a wedding, "reimbursable". What you spend on belongs in Expense Accounts, not Tags.
  • Merge duplicates as soon as you spot them, so a Party's page shows everything.

How do I add a Party?

Open Classification and choose New Party. When a Quick Add's Draft names a Party you don't have yet, Velora creates it for you once you post the Draft.

How do I see how much I spend with a Party?

Open the Party. Its page shows what you've spent with it, or received from it, all time and this month, by month for the last year, and every Transaction. Search its Transactions opens them on Transactions with the Party filter set.

Asha's Nature's Basket page shows ₹9,800 a month on average, and that most of it goes to Groceries.

How do I group a trip's spending?
  1. Open the Tags tab and choose New Tag, such as "Goa trip".
  2. Tick it on each Split for the trip: hotel, flights, meals.
  3. Open the Tag to see what the trip cost in all, whichever Expense Account each Split went to.
How do I fix two Parties that are really one?

Open the one you don't want, choose More actions, then Merge into another, and pick the one to keep. Everything recorded with the first now has the second, and the first is gone.

Asha has both "Natures Basket" and "Nature's Basket". Merging the first into the second moves its Transactions and Schedules across.

What happens if I try to delete a Party I've used?

Velora won't delete a Party that Transactions, Drafts or Schedules still use, as it would leave them without one. Merge it into another, or Hide it to keep it off new Transactions while its history stays.

What happens if I delete a Tag I've used?

Velora tells you how many Splits carry it, and offers to Merge it into another Tag or to Force delete it. Force delete takes the Tag off every Split; the Transactions themselves stay.

What happens if I hide a Party or Tag?

It leaves the list and is no longer offered on new Transactions. Past Transactions keep it, and you can still filter Transactions by it. Find it again under Hidden at the foot of the list, and Unhide it any time.

{% endblock %} {% block terms %}
Party
Someone outside you that money is paid to or received from, such as a shop, employer, landlord or friend. The same Party can be on money in and money out.
Tag
A name of your own you put on Splits to group them across Parties and Accounts, such as a trip. It plays no part in Budgets.
Split
One part of a Transaction, moving an amount from one Account to another. Tags go on Splits, so one bill can be partly tagged.
Merge
Folding one Party into another, or one Tag into another. The first disappears and everything that pointed at it points at the second.
