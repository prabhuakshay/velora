A Draft is a Transaction that waits for you before it is recorded. Velora makes Drafts for you: from a Quick Add line you type, from a Schedule on its due date, for a card's Statement, a SIP, a Card EMI's interest, or a row of an Investment Import. A Draft changes no Balance until you post it, so nothing reaches your records without you seeing it first.

Quick Add turns a short line of plain text, such as "lunch at Toit 850 on hdfc card", into a Draft, using an AI.

{% endblock %} {% block setup %}
  • Use Quick Add for everyday spending. Type it when you pay, and check the Draft when you next sit down.
  • Clear the Drafts page once a day. The badge on Drafts counts what is waiting. Post what's right, edit what's nearly right, reject what's wrong.
  • Name the Account the way you'd say it. "on icici card" or "from cash" helps the AI pick the right one.
  • Start a Draft by hand for money you expect but can't record yet, such as a refund you've been promised. It needs only a date.

How do I write a Quick Add?

Type a line in the Quick Add box at the top of Drafts and choose Add. On other pages, the floating + button opens the same box. On the Drafts page, press / to jump to it.

Asha types "dinner at Truffles 1200 on icici card". A moment later a Draft appears: Party "Truffles", From "ICICI card", To "Eating out", ₹1,200, dated today.

Say who, how much and which Account. Add a date when it wasn't today, such as "yesterday" or "on 3 Oct".

How do I post a Draft?

Choose Post on it. To change something first, choose Edit: Save and post records it, and Save Draft keeps it waiting, gaps and all, for later.

How do I post everything that's ready?

Choose Post all ready above the list.

Why didn't Post all ready post this Draft?

It posts only Drafts dated today or earlier that are complete and not estimates. A Draft is left waiting when:

  • it is missing an amount or an Account,
  • its amount is an estimate, such as a power bill Velora guessed from last month, or a card payment before you've entered the real Statement Amount,
  • it is dated after today.

Open it, fill in what's missing, and choose Save and post.

What happens if I post a Draft dated next week?

It is recorded on today's date, not next week's: posting it says the money has moved now. A Draft dated ahead says "Posts today, not" its date to remind you. Post all ready leaves it to wait for its day.

How do I start a Draft by hand?

Choose Start by hand under the Quick Add box, or New Draft when Quick Add is off. Only the date is needed now; add the amounts and Accounts when you post it.

A friend promises to pay Asha back ₹1,500 next Friday. Asha starts a Draft dated that Friday with the friend as its Party, and posts it when the money arrives.

What happens when a Quick Add goes wrong?

While the AI works, the Quick Add shows as Processing ("Drafting…"). If it can't make a valid Draft, it shows as Failed with the reason. You can then:

  • Resubmit it with edited text,
  • Retry as is, to send the same text again,
  • Discard it.

One still Processing after five minutes is stuck, so it offers Retry and Discard too.

What happens if I reject a Draft from a Schedule?

That one Occurrence is marked Skipped, never Missed, and the Schedule carries on as before. Rejecting is how you skip one month of something that repeats.

Why is there no Quick Add box?

Quick Add needs an AI service, which whoever runs your Velora sets up. Without one, the Drafts page shows New Draft instead, and Drafts from Schedules, Statements and imports still arrive as usual.

How much does the AI cost?

Open AI cost and accuracy at the foot of the Drafts page. It shows this month's cost and the cost so far, in US dollars, retries and failures included, and how often a Draft was posted without edits.

{% endblock %} {% block terms %}
Draft
A Transaction not yet posted. It shows where it came from, needs only a date until you post it, and changes no Balance until then.
Quick Add
A short line of text you write about a money event, which an AI reads to propose a Draft.
Processing
A Quick Add the AI is still working on. One still Processing after five minutes can be retried or discarded.
Failed
A Quick Add the AI couldn't turn into a valid Draft. Retry it, resubmit it with new text, or discard it.
Posted
A Quick Add whose Draft you recorded as a Transaction, with or without edits.
Rejected
A Quick Add you dropped. It is kept, but no longer waits for you.
Discard
Dropping a Failed Quick Add, which marks it Rejected.
AI call
One request Velora makes to the AI for a Quick Add, kept with what it cost.
