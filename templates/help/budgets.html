A Budget is the most you plan to spend under one Expense Account in a month, such as ₹12,000 on Groceries. Velora compares it with what you've Spent so far and what's still expected, so you can see how the month is going at a glance. An Overall Budget does the same for everything you spend in a month.

{% endblock %} {% block setup %}
  • Budget only what you want to watch. Groceries, Eating out and Shopping need a Budget; Rent, which is the same every month, doesn't. What you Spend on them still shows as Unbudgeted.
  • Start from what you really spend. Each Expense Account's page shows its Monthly average; set the Budget a little below that.
  • Set an Overall Budget for everything you spend in a month, including Rent and anything Unbudgeted. It shows whether the month works as a whole.
  • Leave a Budget in place. It carries on every month until you change it, so you set it once.

How do I set a Budget?

Open Budgets. Under Add a Budget, type an amount next to the Expense Account. It saves as you go. To change it, type a new amount; to remove it, empty the box.

Asha sets Groceries ₹12,000, Eating out ₹6,000 and Shopping ₹5,000, and an Overall Budget of ₹60,000 out of an ₹85,000 salary.

How do I read a Budget?

Each row shows what's Spent so far as a solid bar, and what's Expected from Schedules and waiting Drafts as a lighter bar after it. A row is marked Close once Spent reaches 90% of its Budget, and Over once it passes it. Expected never changes the mark, only Spent does.

By 20 October Asha has Spent ₹10,900 of the ₹12,000 Groceries Budget: 91%, so it shows Close with ₹1,100 left.

Why doesn't my Budget stop me from spending?

A Budget is the most you plan to spend, which you measure against, not money set aside. Velora never refuses a Transaction because a Budget is used up, and it doesn't move money around to cover it. Going over shows as Over, and the morning email tells you once when a Budget is Close and once when it is Over.

What happens to what I didn't spend last month?

Nothing. Each month starts fresh at the same Budget: what's left over or overspent never carries into the next month.

What happens if I change a Budget in the middle of the month?

The new amount applies to the whole of this month, back to the 1st, and every month after. Earlier months keep the amount they had.

How do I look at last month?

Use the arrows beside the month name. Past months are read-only: you can change only this month's Budgets.

Does a Refund count?

Yes. A Refund lowers what was Spent under its Expense Account in the month it comes back, which can take Spent below zero.

Do my SIP and card payment count as spending?

No. A SIP moves money into an Investment Account, and paying the card moves money from HDFC Savings to the card: both are between your own Accounts. The spending on the card counted on the day of each purchase.

Why do my Budgets add up to more than the Overall Budget?

Velora allows it and says so on the page. Budgets are each the most you'd spend on one thing; the Overall Budget is what you'd spend in all, and you rarely hit every Budget in the same month.

{% endblock %} {% block terms %}
Budget
The most you plan to spend under one Expense Account in a calendar month. It holds every month until you change it.
Overall Budget
The most you plan to spend across all Expense Accounts in a month, Unbudgeted included.
Spent
What went out under an Expense Account in a month through posted Transactions, less Refunds that month.
Expected
What Upcoming Occurrences and waiting Drafts dated in the month are set to add to Spent once posted. It never counts as Spent.
Unbudgeted
What you Spent under Expense Accounts that have no Budget.
Refund
Money coming back from an Expense Account, which lowers what was Spent under it.
